Restock for iPhone: Support

Install

Get Restock from the App Store. Open it and start adding items. There is no account or sign-in.

Backups

Your lists live only on your iPhone. To keep a copy, open More and tap Export JSON or Export CSV, then save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. Back up from time to time, and always before deleting the app or changing phones.

Reminders

After you check an item off a few times on a steady schedule, Restock learns how often you buy it and reminds you at 9:00 AM, 1 to 3 days before it's due. Allow notifications when Restock asks. If you said no, turn them on in Settings > Notifications > Restock.

Widget

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Restock. Small, medium and large sizes are available, plus Lock Screen widgets. The widget shows what you need now and what's coming due. Tap it to open Restock.

Restore

New iPhone or reinstalled app: download Restock again from your App Store purchases at no charge. Then open More, tap Import, and choose your backup file.

Purchase problems

Restock is a one-time App Store purchase. For billing or refund questions, use Apple's Report a Problem at reportaproblem.apple.com.
