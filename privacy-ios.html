Restock for iPhone: Privacy & Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Data not collected

Restock has no accounts and no server. Your lists, purchase history, recurring items and sets are stored only on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive, see, sell or share any of it. There are no ads, analytics, trackers or third-party SDKs.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS itself. Nothing is sent to us to make them work.

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app can erase it. Use Export in More to keep a backup file. Where that file goes is up to you.

Voice entry uses Apple's keyboard dictation or speech recognition, which Apple provides under its own privacy policy.

Purchase

Restock is a one-time purchase through the App Store. It does not expire and is not billed again.

Terms

Restock is licensed to you under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Items, dates and suggestions are generated from what you enter and are meant as a convenience. Restock is provided as is, and we may improve the app over time.
